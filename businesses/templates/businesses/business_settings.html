Client Payment Controls

Choose what clients can do themselves and what owners can charge only after explicit customer authorization.

Client card payments

Allow customers to pay invoices and estimate deposits by credit card when Stripe is connected.

Default card checkout on invoices

New invoices use this as the starting point. The invoice detail page still has a per-invoice card payment toggle.

Card processing fee

Optionally add a disclosed fee only when a customer chooses credit card checkout. Confirm this is allowed for your state and card network rules.

{{ form.card_processing_fee_percent }} {{ form.card_processing_fee_percent.errors }}

Default is 3%. The invoice total stays unchanged; this fee is added only to the Stripe card checkout amount.

Saved cards on file

Allow authorized customer cards to be saved and used for customer-level auto-charge rules.

Customer-level control

Auto-charge is turned on per customer from the client billing tab, with separate options for completed services and monthly invoices.

Invoice-level control

Each invoice can still allow or block client card checkout, even when global card payments are enabled.
